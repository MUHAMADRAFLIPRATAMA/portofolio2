About me

  • University UMY
  • Location Yogyakarta
  • Focus Networking & Software Engineering

I am a Universitas Muhammadiyah Yogyakarta student who is curious about new things, especially networking and software engineering. To me, these two fields complement each other. No matter how good an application is, it is useless if the network beneath it does not work, and a well-designed network only matters when people can use the applications it supports.

During my studies, I have explored different areas, from designing intercity networks in Cisco Packet Tracer to building Android applications with Jetpack Compose. I am currently learning to build polished products from start to finish. I am open to internships or collaborative projects that offer room to learn while working on real-world problems.
